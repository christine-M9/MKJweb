Author of the Following Books

My publications support learners within the 8-4-4 curriculum while also exploring African identity, history, and storytelling.

Epic History – Comprehensive (Paper 1 & 2)

8-4-4 Curriculum

Author: Mathew Juma

A complete History & Government textbook covering both Paper 1 and Paper 2 of the Kenyan 8-4-4 curriculum. The book presents world history, African history, and Kenyan historical developments from pre-colonial times to the post-independence era. It includes timelines, maps, explanations, and exam practice questions to help learners prepare effectively for KCSE.

Epic History – Paper 1

8-4-4 Curriculum

Author: Mathew Juma

A dedicated Paper 1 textbook concentrating on Kenyan history and government. It includes source-based questions, essay writing guidance, and detailed coverage of colonialism in Kenya, resistance movements, constitutional development, and post-independence governance. Ideal for exam-focused study.

Epic History – Paper 2

8-4-4 Curriculum

Author: Mathew Juma

This book focuses on History & Government Paper 2 under the 8-4-4 syllabus. It covers major world and African history topics such as imperialism, independence movements, and global political developments. The content is structured to build understanding through clear explanations, examples, and revision questions.

Fate of Africa

Novel

Author: Mathew Juma

A historical novel that explores Africa’s struggles, identity, and destiny. Through storytelling, the book reflects on leadership, conflict, culture, and resilience across the continent. It blends history with fiction to present thought-provoking themes about Africa’s past, present, and future.
